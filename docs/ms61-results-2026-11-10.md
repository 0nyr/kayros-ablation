# ms61 results, 2026-11-10

Development evidence from manuscript session 61. Solver candidates are commits of the worktree `kayros-ms61` (branch `ms61/bpc-control`). Harness usage is in [ms61-harness.md](ms61-harness.md). Every run below is a single cold BPC run with CPLEX 12.10 on one pinned core with a 16 GiB limit and a 3,600 s limit unless stated. Every closure was re-checked with the pinned checker and compared across arms and with the frozen references. No summary reported a soundness alert.

## The comparison changed during the day

The [partial-dominance defect](ms61-partial-dominance-defect.md) found at 19:00 is in upstream Lera-Romero's code and in every Kayros build before `86f74ad`. It removes labels, so both solvers did less work than a correct pricer. All timings measured before the fix are therefore kept as a record only. The comparison that counts is fixed against fixed: Kayros `86f74ad` or later against the repaired control f3 (`1001ff2`, fixed Lera `f2c9e20` plus the same line).

Effect of the fix on Lera itself, same grvingt cohort, 37 instances: closures 32 before and 32 after, time ratio f3 to f2 of 1.04 in geometric mean over 24 instances. Individual instances move a lot in both directions: C104 from 2,252 s to 3,412 s, R104 from 2,392 s to the limit, R107 from the limit to 2,711 s. At n=100 no proven optimum changed: the 33 instances closed both before and after the fix agree on every value.

## Before the fix (record only)

- Screens 1 to 3 (gros, 10 instances): the control levers, the packed filter and the relaxation closed C104 in 318 to 931 s against 2,120 to 2,234 s for fixed Lera f2. The relaxation's share of that gain did not survive the fix.
- Broad waves 1 and 2 (grvingt, 56 instances): Lera f2 closed 32, the Kayros candidates 33, with capped means 1,801.9 s (Lera), 1,793.4 s, 1,753.3 s and 1,739.9 s. The 19 instances R202 to R211, RC202 to RC208, C203 and C204 never got past the root for any arm.
- Execution measurements with identical search work remain valid as relative costs: packed dominance filter, 358 s to 196 s per Exact pass on C104 with correction on. Build flags, 172 s to 143 s.

## Soundness evidence on the fixed build

- Dabia2013 n=25, 56 instances, 3 settings with the verification pass: 305 passes, 0 catches.
- Dabia2013 n=50, 56 instances, 2 settings with the verification pass at a 60 s limit: 67 closures, 193 passes, 0 catches.
- Relaxation re-check (verification bit 128), n=25: 157 passes, 0 catches.
- Repository BPC soundness suite: 56 passed, 3 opt-in skipped. Opt-in randomized sweep: 21 cases, none unsound.
- n=100: 33 instances closed before and after the fix, all values equal.
- Other families, pre-fix build against fixed build at a 45 s limit on small instances: Vu2020 n=59 (56 closed by both), Ari2018 n=30 and n=40 (12), Rifki2020 n=30 and n=40 (4). No proven optimum differs. This is an absence of observed change on a sample at a short limit, not a clearance of earlier certificates.
