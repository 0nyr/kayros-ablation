An initial development attempt without explicit affinity is retained separately and excluded from matched comparisons.

`scripts/validate_upstream_smoke.py` checks the final witness against the corresponding MAMUT n25 fixture using the pinned v0.11 checker, omitting the native declared cost from the checker input. It retains native-minus-checked cost discrepancies. Validation has no recorded timing. The original main program and BCP retain their original timing and termination control flow. They lack process-launch events, safe incremental global-bound events and a closure census. ## Development observations

The fixed-affinity 2 s native C101_25 and C101_50 runs both produced checker-valid complete witnesses. The v0.11 costs were 24,709.177157546477 and 49,242.92185601275. Native-minus-checked differences were approximately -3.64e-12 and -7.28e-12. These are development observations without proof credit or performance interpretation. Raw records remain in ignored `outputs/ms56/upstream-*` directories.

An independent IGP integration audit compared each materialized ATF knot and segment midpoint against the original distance, speed-zone and cluster-speed records for these 2 fixtures. Demands, service times, time windows, capacities, horizons and coordinates agree exactly after removing the duplicated end depot. The maximum finite arrival-time discrepancy was approximately 3.64e-12 over 11,810 samples for n25 and 46,334 for n50. The materialized ATFs additionally contain 650 and 2,550 sampled departures whose arrivals lie beyond the native speed horizon. The native integrator reports those arrivals as unavailable, whereas MAMUT extends the final speed zone. The common TDVRPTW depot-arrival horizon excludes these late arrivals. Preserve this domain distinction in the full equivalence audit. This evidence supports the intended customer/time-scale mapping and identifies the late-domain difference, while leaving all-56 equivalence and floating-point pricing behavior for the pilot gate.

## Phase 2 instrumentation and full-panel comparability

`patches/upstream-0002-phase2-events.patch` applies directly to original commit `cabc56ad9cdb22bed81a8a31f1671d40152055e6`, independently of the earlier single-thread smoke patch. It adds process-launch monotonic timestamps, incumbent events, root/frontier native bound events, termination events and a compact CG closure census. Resource-deadline guards keep the final column-generation LP re-solve, strong-branching LPs, cut separation and freeze heuristic inside the common remaining budget. Interrupted branching cannot silently close the tree. Successful native pricing and route arithmetic remain unchanged. The patch also records restricted-master infeasibility and fractional parents without a branching arc as source diagnostics. These diagnostics identify cases requiring explanation under the solver's declared conventions. They are not themselves a demonstrated wrong objective value.

The source audit corrected the earlier cut-threshold recommendation: the chosen immutable upstream source uses `epsilon_bigger(best_violation, 0.1)` at lines 329–330 of `code/src/bcp/bcp.cpp`. Its raw source SHA-256 is `22f7c76ccd7dd704371b9f54ef8d2ffbe89f57d546c0edd2b89e6c2d684fd53d`. [The source audit](../configs/upstream-source-audit.json) records this evidence. Preserve 0.1 across upstream and Kayros. A generic lineage statement about positive-violation separation does not override this selected source pin.

The matched CPLEX settings are 1 thread, primal and dual LP feasibility tolerances of 1e-7, integrality tolerance of 1e-6 and MIP relative gap 0 for the freeze heuristic. Native events report the first effective LP and MIP configuration after internal overrides, including version, method, presolve, reduction, scaling, parallel mode and random seed. `scripts/upstream_inventory.py` hashes the binary, runtime libraries, original native input files, experiment configuration, complete source tree, CPLEX archive and instrumentation patch. The common launcher uses `scripts/upstream_event_worker.py` with `runtime_kind: native`, `UPSTREAM_PROBE_BINARY` and `UPSTREAM_SOURCE_ROOT` from that immutable manifest. The worker applies the same CPU affinity, address-space cap, process-launch clock and post-run checking boundary as the Kayros worker.

The full frozen n100 panel passed `scripts/audit_upstream_panel.py` under the pinned v0.11 loader on a reserved compute node. All 56 identities and ATF artifacts match the frozen manifest hashes. Demands, service times, time windows, capacities, vehicle counts, horizons and coordinates match the native records exactly after depot mapping, with no missing native arcs. The audit tested the union of original IGP breakpoints and materialized ATF knots, together with intervening midpoints. Across 10,340,020 finite samples, the maximum arrival-time discrepancy was approximately 3.64e-12 and no sample exceeded 1e-8. Every sample unavailable under the native speed horizon would arrive outside the shared horizon. This establishes numerical representation agreement under the declared computational conventions, without claiming bit-identical floating-point route accumulation.
