Tiny floating-point differences remain visible diagnostics and do not alone block the pilot.

## Approved global prefix selection

Compare 15, 60, 180, 600 and 1,800 s with 3 paired ILS seeds (42, 123, 456), 12 fixed identities and one 3,600 s total horizon per cell. First invalidate every affected candidate configuration if a correctness defect is discovered. Repair and rerun the paired affected development cells under a new manifest before ranking.

Rank valid candidates lexicographically by (1) minimum restricted mean time to accepted proof over all 36 cells, treating every unproved cell as 3,600 s, (2) minimum mean primal Λ over the same denominator, then (3) shorter prefix duration. Calculate within-instance means before the equal-instance aggregate. No feasible-only denominator or per-instance prefix choice is allowed. Keep final squeezed gap and feasibility as descriptive secondary outcomes. Report accepted proof count as a diagnostic alongside restricted mean proof time, without using it as a primary ranking key. This rule prioritizes proof speed while making incumbent quality a prespecified tie-break. It does not claim independent tuning-validation evidence. Select the final prefix after pilot review under this approved rule. Prefix deadlines are measured from process launch, including setup.
